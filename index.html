Dual-Criteria Overload Detection System

Detects safe limits breaches by simultaneously evaluating passenger headcount AND gross vehicle weight.

Level 1: Safe Normal
Load < 80% Capacity

When passenger headcount (< 40 pax) and weight (< 2,800 kg) are safely below threshold. Status indicator is green, LCD displays STATUS: NORMAL, and entry doors remain open.

Level 2: Warning Buffer
Load ≥ 80% Threshold

When passenger count (≥ 40 pax) OR weight (≥ 2,800 kg) breaches the 80% safety margin. Warning chime fires, LCD displays STATUS: WARNING to notify conductor to prepare to halt boarding.

Level 3: Critical Overload
Load > 100% Certified Limit

Breached when passenger count exceeds 50 pax OR gross weight exceeds 3,500 kg. High-intensity 2.4 kHz piezo buzzer sounds continuously, doors interlock, and emergency audit incident is logged.
