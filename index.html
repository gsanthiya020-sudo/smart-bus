Overload Detection & Incident Management

Automatic alert evaluation based on dual parameters: passenger headcount & payload weight.

Condition 1: Normal
Load < 80% Capacity

When passenger count and weight are strictly within normal boundaries (< 80% threshold). Status light is green and LCD shows STATUS: NORMAL.

Condition 2: Warning
Load ≥ 80% Limit

When passenger count OR weight reaches 80% of configured safe limit. Orange alert displays "BUS APPROACHING CAPACITY" to advise conductor.

Condition 3: Overload
Load > 100% Limit

When count or weight breaches safe capacity. Red warning triggers, piezo buzzer sounds at 2.4 kHz, and event is logged for transport audit.
